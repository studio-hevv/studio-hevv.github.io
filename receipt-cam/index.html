How to make and save receipt photos

  1. Capture a momentOpen Receipt Roll and press the shutter. Your photo appears as a black-and-white receipt on the screen; pull it down to tear it off.
  2. Review your rollOpen the roll archive to revisit your receipt photos. Open an individual receipt and tap [RAW] to view the original color photo.
  3. Save to your photo albumTap [SAVE] on an individual receipt, or sign and tap [SAVE ALL] on the daily settlement screen to save the whole day.

Key Experience

  • Thermal printing & the pull-down tear-off Press the shutter and the receipt rattles out of the slot. Pull it down and tear it off — haptic feedback makes the act of printing feel vividly real.
  • Atkinson 1-bit dithering A precise recreation of the monochrome thermal paper texture and the classic pixel typography of 1990s POS receipt printers.
  • On-device AI itemization & location stamp On-device AI turns the objects and moments in your photo into witty receipt line items, and stamps the name of the place where the shot was taken.
  • End-of-day 'TODAY'S TOTAL' & signature At night, the day's shots close out into a single continuous receipt roll. Sign with your finger and save the whole day to your photo album.
  • Your photos stay on your phone No sign-up, no cloud upload. Photos and location never leave your device — image processing and AI itemization run entirely on-device.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when I use up my 24 shots for the day?

Like a film camera, the shutter closes once you have used all 24 shots for the day. A fresh roll of 24 shots is automatically loaded every morning at 09:00.

Are my photos sent to an external server?

No. All image processing and AI itemization run locally on your device, and your photos never leave it.

Is any usage data collected?

Photos and location are never sent. The app does use Google Firebase for usage analytics and crash diagnostics, and for measuring install-ad performance — usage events and device information only, never photo content. See the Privacy Policy.

Where are the receipt images stored?

They are kept in the app's roll archive. Tap [SAVE] on an individual receipt, or sign and tap [SAVE ALL] on the daily settlement screen, to save them to your phone's Photos app.

Can I see the original color photo?

Yes. Open a receipt in the archive and tap the [RAW] button to view the original color photo and save it to your album.
