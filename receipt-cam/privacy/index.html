Privacy Policy

Article 1 (General Provisions)

Studio HEVV ("Operator") complies with relevant privacy laws in operating the iOS and Android application 'Receipt Camera' ("App"). This policy outlines the scope and purpose of the information the App collects and uses.

Article 2 (Information Collected & Methods)

Most of your data, including photos and location, is stored only on your device. The App does, however, use third-party services for usage analytics, crash diagnostics, and advertising measurement, and the limited information described in items 5 through 7 below is transmitted externally for those purposes (see Article 4).

  1. Camera: Used to capture photos and generate receipt-style images. Captured photos remain solely in the App's on-device storage. The recognition that turns objects in your photo into receipt line items runs entirely on the device (Vision on iOS, ML Kit on Android); the image is never transmitted externally.
  2. Photo Library: Write access only, used to save finished receipt images to your album when you ask for it. Your existing photos are never read.
  3. Location: An optional feature that stamps the capture location onto receipts. Only approximate (city/district-level) location is used, and the coordinates are converted to a place-name string on the device. Neither the coordinates nor the place name is transmitted externally.
  4. App Settings: Your preferences, such as notification consent and whether the location stamp is on, are stored strictly on the device (UserDefaults on iOS; SharedPreferences and an on-device database on Android).
  5. Usage analytics: Google Firebase Analytics records three events — receipt printed, photo saved, roll saved — plus screen views, with no additional parameters. Firebase's automatically collected baseline events (app opens, sessions, and similar) along with device model, OS, and app version are included. No photo content is included.
  6. Crash diagnostics: Google Firebase Crashlytics transmits diagnostic information — stack trace, device model, OS version, app version — when the App terminates abnormally. It contains no information that identifies you personally.
  7. Advertising measurement: Uses an advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android) together with app install and usage events. On Android, the Google Play install referrer is also read. Handling differs by platform; see Article 4.

Article 3 (Purpose of Use)

  1. Camera — Photo capture and receipt rendering
  2. Photo Library write — Saving finished receipts to your album
  3. Location — Optional location stamp on receipts
  4. Notifications — Daily 22:00 reminder to save today's receipt, daily 09:00 fresh-roll notification
  5. Usage analytics — Understanding how features are used and improving the service
  6. Crash diagnostics — Diagnosing abnormal terminations and improving stability
  7. Advertising measurement — Measuring the performance of install advertising

Article 4 (Third-Party Sharing & External Transfer)

The App uses the following third-party services, which process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics — usage analytics and crash diagnostics (iOS and Android). Privacy Policy
  • Meta (Facebook) SDK — app-install advertising measurement. Privacy Policy

Advertising identifiers are handled differently on each platform.

  • iOS: Your advertising identifier (IDFA) is used for cross-app tracking only if you allow it in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can withdraw consent at any time in [Settings] > [Privacy & Security] > [Tracking].
  • Android: There is no equivalent system prompt, so the Advertising ID is collected by default. You can stop this by choosing "Delete advertising ID" in [Settings] > [Google] > [Ads].

Your photos and location are never transmitted externally under any circumstances, and the Operator does not sell your personal information. The services above may run on servers outside your country, so the related information may be transferred abroad.

Article 5 (Data Retention & Deletion)

On-device data exists solely on your device; deleting the App permanently removes all associated local data. Images you saved to your photo album are managed directly by you. Analytics, diagnostic, and advertising data held by the third parties above is retained under their own retention policies.

Article 6 (User Rights)

  1. iOS: Change Camera, Location, Photos, and Notification permissions anytime in [Settings] > [Receipt Camera]. Ad-tracking consent is changed in [Settings] > [Privacy & Security] > [Tracking].
  2. Android: Change Camera, Location, and Notification permissions anytime in [Settings] > [Apps] > [Receipt Camera] > [Permissions]. The Advertising ID can be deleted or reset in [Settings] > [Google] > [Ads].
  3. Deleting the App instantly purges all local app data.
  4. For inquiries, contact us at contact@studio-hevv.com.

Article 7 (Contact)

  • Operator: Studio HEVV
  • Email: contact@studio-hevv.com

Article 8 (Effective Date)

This policy is effective as of August 26, 2026.

第2条（收集的信息与方式）

包括照片与位置在内的绝大部分数据仅保存在您的设备中。但本应用会为使用情况分析、崩溃诊断与广告效果衡量使用第三方服务，在此过程中下列第5至7项的有限信息会传输至外部（参见第4条）。

  1. 相机：用于拍摄照片并生成小票风格图像。拍摄的照片仅保存在本应用的设备内存储中。将照片中的物件转换为小票品项的识别处理全部在设备内完成（iOS 使用 Vision，Android 使用 ML Kit），图像不会传输至外部。
  2. 照片相册：仅使用写入权限，在您请求时将完成的小票图像保存到相册。绝不读取您已有的照片。
  3. 位置：在小票上标注拍摄地点的可选功能。仅使用大致位置（市/区级别），坐标在设备内转换为地名文本。坐标与地名均不会传输至外部。
  4. 应用设置：通知同意、是否启用位置戳等设置仅保存在设备内（iOS 为 UserDefaults，Android 为 SharedPreferences 及设备内数据库）。
  5. 使用情况分析：通过 Google Firebase Analytics 收集小票打印、照片保存、胶卷保存三类事件及屏幕浏览记录，不附带额外参数。其中包含 Firebase 自动收集的基础事件（应用启动、会话等）以及设备型号、系统与应用版本信息。不包含照片内容。
  6. 崩溃诊断：通过 Google Firebase Crashlytics，在本应用异常终止时发送堆栈跟踪、设备型号、系统版本、应用版本等诊断信息。不包含可识别个人身份的信息。
  7. 广告效果衡量：使用广告标识符（iOS 为 IDFA，Android 为广告 ID）以及应用安装与使用事件。在 Android 上还会读取 Google Play 安装来源（安装引荐来源）。处理方式因平台而异，详见第4条。

Artigo 2.º (Informações Recolhidas e Métodos)

A maior parte dos seus dados, incluindo fotografias e localização, é guardada apenas no seu dispositivo. No entanto, a Aplicação utiliza serviços de terceiros para análise de utilização, diagnóstico de falhas e medição publicitária, e as informações limitadas descritas nos pontos 5 a 7 são transmitidas para o exterior para esses fins (ver Artigo 4.º).

  3. Localização: Funcionalidade opcional que carimba no recibo o local da captura. É usada apenas a localização aproximada (ao nível da cidade/freguesia) e as coordenadas são convertidas num texto com o nome do local no próprio dispositivo. Nem as coordenadas nem o nome do local são transmitidos para o exterior.
  4. Definições da Aplicação: As suas preferências, como o consentimento de notificações e a ativação do carimbo de localização, são guardadas estritamente no dispositivo (UserDefaults no iOS; SharedPreferences e uma base de dados local no Android).
  5. Análise de utilização: O Google Firebase Analytics regista três eventos — recibo impresso, fotografia guardada, rolo guardado — além das visualizações de ecrã, sem parâmetros adicionais. Estão incluídos os eventos de base recolhidos automaticamente pelo Firebase (aberturas da aplicação, sessões e semelhantes), bem como o modelo do dispositivo e as versões do sistema e da aplicação. Não é incluído qualquer conteúdo fotográfico.
  6. Diagnóstico de falhas: O Google Firebase Crashlytics transmite informações de diagnóstico — rastreio de pilha, modelo do dispositivo, versão do sistema, versão da aplicação — quando a Aplicação termina de forma anómala. Não contém informações que o identifiquem pessoalmente.
  7. Medição publicitária: Utiliza um identificador de publicidade (IDFA no iOS, ID de publicidade no Android) juntamente com eventos de instalação e utilização. No Android, é também lido o referenciador de instalação do Google Play. O tratamento difere consoante a plataforma; ver Artigo 4.º.
