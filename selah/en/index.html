What is Selah?

A sermon can move you in the moment and fade by the time you get home. Selah helps you write the message down as you hear it, then open it again to reflect. Church notes, sermon summaries, devotions and prayer notes all live in one place.

How to use it

  1. Write it down in the moment
    During worship, write down the verses and thoughts that speak to you.
  2. Come back and reflect
    Open your saved notes again and reflect on the message.
  3. Live it out
    Build your own archive of the message and carry it into your week.

Frequently asked questions

What is Selah?

Where can I get it?
